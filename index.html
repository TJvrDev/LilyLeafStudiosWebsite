Meet TJ

TJ's Great Adventure is a storybook third-person adventure starring a small hero with a big backpack. Explore hand-crafted worlds packed with secrets, outsmart giant bosses, and collect the treasures that open the way forward.

Every corner of the map is built to be explored: swaying jungles, roaring rivers, lava-filled caverns, snowy peaks and a village full of friends who need your help.

What awaits

🪃

Find legendary gear

Earn a boomerang, a shield and a glider. Each one opens up new paths through worlds you thought you'd finished.

🐉

Face epic bosses

A giant spider, a temple snake, a pirate crew and more. Every chapter builds to a showdown.

🏡

Build up the Village

Make friends with the villagers, earn their trophies, and watch the Village come alive as your adventure grows.

🏂

Play the mini games

Snowboard down the mountain, race a glider through rings over the lava, play volleyball and more.

💎

Fast travel

Find the travel crystals to jump between worlds and go back for anything you missed.

⭐

Your kind of challenge

Relax on Easy, or test yourself on Hard and Ultimate, where every hit counts.
